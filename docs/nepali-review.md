# Nepali text for review

**Status: written by Claude, not yet read by a native speaker.** Please read the Nepali next to its English and tell us what is wrong, unnatural or too stiff. The English is the source of meaning.

TANGO is a flood-response tool. The readers are district officers, rescue coordinators and responders, so the Nepali should be plain, formal and unambiguous, the register of an official situation report. Numbers and place names are from the Trishuli corridor run (16 and 28 August 2026) and should stay as they are.

How to reply: for each problem, send the item (the heading and the line) and your wording, for example `Cut-off settlements, line 1: "..." should be "..."`. Any note on a term used throughout (see the list of terms below) is the most useful of all. Texts were copied from the live site, <https://bhoopesh282008.github.io/Tango/>, in the language switch's English and नेपाली settings.
